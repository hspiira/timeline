# CFO Financial Assessment

**Date:** 2026-08-29
**FX basis:** 1 USD = 3,772 UGX (XE mid-market, retrieved 29 Aug 2026 15:29 UTC). **Not a Bank of Uganda official rate**; the BoU site did not render. Verify against BoU's published opening rate before any board or contractual use.

**Rating: 3/10 on financial merit.**

## Executive summary

1. **Nominated vertical: clinical and health-research records integrity** (NDA-regulated trials plus donor-audited research institutions). The only candidate with a current, written, verifiable mandate carrying a long retention obligation and a USD-funded payer.
2. **Runner-up: BoU-supervised Tier 1-3 lenders.** Real budgets, real AML mandate, but a sales cycle a pre-revenue solo founder cannot survive.
3. **SACCOs / Tier 4 microfinance: killed on price.** Verified ceiling for full core-banking software in Uganda is **UGX 840,000/year (approximately USD 223)** for unlimited members, with at least seven local incumbents already there.
4. **Coffee / EUDR: killed.** Dates pushed to 30 Dec 2026 / 30 Jun 2027; the December 2025 amendment removed the due-diligence-statement burden from downstream operators; UCDA was abolished into MAAIF on 23 Dec 2024; and government is building the national traceability system with public money.
5. **NGO / donor-audit: killed.** Uganda lost approximately **USD 307m, about 66% of its USAID programme**; EU cut EUR 2bn; GIZ programmes suspended Oct 2025.
6. **Verified willingness-to-pay for adjacent Ugandan software is USD 111 to 223 per year.** That single fact governs everything below.
7. Burn: approximately **USD 1,250/month solo**, approximately **USD 3,500/month** with one engineer and part-time sales. Twelve months to about 10 customers is approximately **USD 28,500**, excluding a likely USD 15k-40k security certification.
8. Realistic **ARR month 12 approximately USD 18,000; month 24 approximately USD 80,000.**
9. Available capital: KAIN USD 10k-100k, UBAN angels, UNCDF/Outbox refundable grants up to USD 40k. **Financing is not the binding constraint. Demand is.**
10. A 3 means: do not fund the roadmap, fund the 90 days of discovery only.

A Ugandan SACCO can buy an entire core-banking system (savings, loans, shares, dividends, payroll, mobile app, unlimited members) for **USD 223 per year**. Timeline is not a core-banking system; it is an integrity layer sitting underneath one. There is no arithmetic in which a layer sells for a multiple of the whole system it augments, to the same buyer.

The Kenya/Uganda spread is instructive: the same category of buyer pays roughly **12x more in Kenya**. That is an argument for Kenya, not for Uganda.

## The general lesson on mandates

> A regulatory mandate is only a willingness-to-pay signal when the state has **not** funded the compliance tooling.

Verified counter-examples in three of four researched verticals: Shs 16bn for a coffee EUDR Coordination Unit and Shs 13.9bn for UCDA certification; NDA's own Clinical Trials Management Information System and UNCST's CRIMS in research; NITA-U's USD 200m Uganda Digital Acceleration Project in government records. **Test this first, every time.**

## The two verticals that survive

### Nominated: clinical and health-research records

The only mandate verified from the primary document itself:

> **National Drug Authority, "Guidelines on the Conduct of Clinical Trials in Uganda", Doc. No. DPS/GDL/024, Revision 1, effective 14 Oct 2024, review due 14 Oct 2027, p.25 of 62:**
> "Documents shall be retained for a minimum period of 20 years in line with the above referenced regulation."
> and: "it shall be the responsibility of both the investigator and the sponsor to ensure the safety of all essential documents related to the trial. Documents may be stored in electronic soft and scanned or hard copies."

A 20-year, electronically-permitted retention obligation, jointly on sponsor and investigator, in force today, pointing at a USD-funded payer.

## ARR arithmetic, every assumption labelled

**Month 12**
- A1 *(assumption)*: 90 days of discovery, first paid pilot signs month 9
- A2 *(assumption)*: 3 paying institutions by month 12. From an estimated 10-15 buyable institutions, 20-25% conversion in year one is aggressive but not absurd for a founder-led sale into a small, well-networked community
- A3 *(estimate, no verified Uganda price point)*: USD 6,000/yr average contract
- **ARR(12) = 3 x 6,000 = USD 18,000 (approx. UGX 67.9m)**

**Month 24**
- A4 *(assumption)*: Uganda saturates at 8 institutions, 4 more from Kenya/Tanzania/Rwanda = 12 total
- A5 *(assumption)*: ACV rises to USD 8,000 as the product hardens and references exist
- ARR(24) = 12 x 8,000 = USD 96,000, less 15% for churn and discounting
- **ARR(24) approximately USD 80,000 (approx. UGX 302m)**

**Read against the cost base.** Scenario 2 burns approximately USD 28,500 in year one. ARR(12) of USD 18,000 does not cover it. ARR(24) of USD 80,000 covers a two-person team with nothing left over. **This is a lifestyle consultancy with a very good codebase attached, not a venture-returns business**, unless regional expansion is far more aggressive than A4 assumes.

## The three financial assumptions that kill this business if wrong

1. **That any Ugandan buyer pays more than approximately USD 2,000/yr for a records-integrity layer.** Verified adjacent price points are USD 111-223/yr. If the true ceiling is USD 300/yr, no Uganda vertical clears even the cost of selling to it. **Highest-probability killer.**

2. **That tamper-evidence is a purchase criterion rather than an unpaid feature.** Zero customer conversations have occurred. If buyers evaluate on workflow parity instead, which is what Ensibuuko, Kwara, CoreTec, SaccoSys, SaccoRa, Ssentezo and FamITech all compete on, then the Merkle trees, integrity epochs, TSA anchoring and chain-repair workflows are capital already destroyed.

3. **That the buyer is not the government, and the government is not building it free.** See [the general lesson on mandates](#the-general-lesson-on-mandates). A free state system sets willingness to pay at zero by construction.

## Ninety-day go / no-go

No further capital after day 90 unless **all five** are true:

| # | Gate | Threshold |
|---|------|-----------|
| 1 | Customer conversations | **30 completed**, of which **at least 15** with a named individual controlling a budget line, all in **one** vertical |
| 2 | Existing spend evidence | **At least 3 buyers state in writing** what they currently pay for an adjacent system, with the figure |
| 3 | Cash, not intent | **At least 1 signed paid pilot at USD 2,000+ annualised.** An LOI is worth nothing and will not be accepted as evidence |
| 4 | Mandate is live | **At least 1 written instance** of an auditor, sponsor, monitor or regulator asking that buyer for tamper-evident, immutable or independently verifiable records |
| 5 | Engineering freeze | **Zero new features shipped in 90 days.** Any engineering hour in this window is itself a fail signal: the risk is demand, and no amount of code reduces it |

Fail gate 1 or 3, **stop.** Fail gate 4, the integrity layer is a hobby: pivot to a plain case-management product and compete on price, or stop.

## Rating: 3/10, defended

**Why not lower.** The asset is real: ~43,500 lines of working backend, a genuine hash-chain plus Merkle plus RFC 3161 stack, and a verified 20-year statutory retention mandate the product happens to fit. Infrastructure cost is genuinely low (approximately USD 150/month verified), the free TSA tier means the marquee feature carries no marginal cost, and total capital at risk to reach a decision is under USD 30,000, inside a single verified KAIN cheque. Cheap optionality on a real regulatory tailwind is worth something.

**Why not higher.** Every commercial input is unverified, and the one price signal that could be verified is roughly one-tenth of what the cost structure requires. Zero customer conversations after building 423 files is a governance failure, not a market condition. Three of the four largest candidate verticals are being displaced by state-funded systems. Donor money contracted sharply in 2025-26. The certification cost required to sell to any regulated buyer roughly equals a full year of runway. And the nominated vertical's institution count, the central sizing input, is an estimate of 10-15 with no register behind it.

**A 3 means: do not fund the roadmap, fund the 90 days of discovery only.** If gates 1-5 clear, this re-rates quickly to a 6. If gate 1 fails, it is a 1, and the correct action is to stop and recover the founder's time.
