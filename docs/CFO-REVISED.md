# CFO REVISED — Ugandan market financial assessment (re-run)

**Date:** 2026-08-29 | **Author:** CFO (re-appointed per `APPOINTMENTS.md`)
**FX basis (secondary, mid-market):** ~UGX 3,700/USD. All conversions approximate. Not a Bank of Uganda official rate.
**Mandate:** Re-baseline market sizing, willingness-to-pay, cost-to-serve and financing. Challenge the prior "killed on price" ruling.

## Executive summary

1. Verified market now sits at **~2,132 licensed money-lending businesses** (UMRA May-2023 list via Daily Monitor, 30 Apr 2025) — roughly **2x** the ~1,050 count the prior team carried.
2. The Tier 4 price ceiling is real and quantified (**UGX 840,000/yr ≈ USD 223**, SaccoRa unlimited members, `[Verified]`), but it governs *full core-banking clone* pricing, not the distinct "loan file of record / court pack" artefact.
3. The artefact prices at **UGX 100k–250k/yr (USD 27–67)** — below the buyer's own statutory cost stack and inside (at the top) the verified WTP band. It does **not** die on price. It dies on **cost-to-sell vs ACV**.
4. **New, load-bearing objection:** the median money-lender loan is **UGX 30k–150k repaid in ≤8 days**; lenders in this mass **do not litigate** under s.88(1) — recovery is by pressure and UMRA mediation (1,000+ disputes handled). **The courtroom wedge the whole thesis rests on may only bind a high-ticket minority.**
5. Financing is available (KAIN USD 10k–50k angel, UNCDF/Outbox 10X grant to USD 40k) but **financing is not the constraint. Demand is.**

## Financing reality

Tracxn (secondary): Uganda raised **USD 12.5m across 4 equity rounds in 2025 (to Nov)** vs USD 2.22m across 9 rounds in the same 2024 window — small and volatile. Realistic 12-month availability: a KAIN angel cheque of **USD 10k–50k**, or a UNCDF/Outbox **10X** refundable grant to **USD 40k** (New Vision, Jun 2026). 12 months to ~10 customers runs ~USD 28.5k per the prior model — inside a single cheque. **Financing is not binding; demand is.**

## Ruling on the Tier 4 price-ceiling question (correction of the prior kill)

Three verified numbers govern:

1. Full core-banking ceiling (SaccoRa, unlimited members): **UGX 840,000/yr ≈ USD 223** — unchanged.
2. The prospective buyer's own statutory stack: **UGX 500,000/yr licence + UGX 200,000/yr dues ≈ USD 186/yr** of spend that already exists.
3. **2,132 licensed money-lending businesses** — real budgets, not bare SACCOs.

**Ruling:** "killed on price" was **wrong as stated** for the *distinct* artefact; it is correct only for full core-banking clone pricing. The loan-record pack is pitched against the compliance/licence budget line, not the operations line that pays SaccoRa, so viable price is **UGX 100k–250k/yr (USD 27–67)**.

**But the ceiling and the pain-distribution together cap this at a low-ACV sidecar:** at ~USD 70 and 2,000 converted, ~**USD 140k ARR**; at ~USD 50 and 500 converted, ~**USD 25k ARR**. **This is not the founding product of a venture-returns company at current ticket sizes.** The operative kill is **cost-to-sell vs ACV**: a USD 50–70 ACV cannot absorb founder-led sales to 2,132 small firms.

**Where the wedge does bind:** the **high-ticket minority that actually litigates and faces statutory inspection** — larger NDTMFIs and the ~15 licensed digital lenders — where ACV can approach **USD 100–150/yr**. Discovery must target this minority, not the 2,132 at large.

## Single best vertical and the objection that matters most

**Best: UMRA money lenders / NDTMFIs** — the only vertical with an end-to-end verified spine (count, statutory schema, court-production obligation, interest-cap frame at 2.8%/month under s.90 / Legal Notice 21/2024, and a real statutory cost stack).

**Biggest objection (must be discovery question #1):** the median loan (UGX 30k–150k, ≤8 days) means the mass market **does not go to court**. The s.88(1) pain this product monetises may not exist for most of the 2,132. **If the honest answer to "what does the documentation problem cost you when you go to court" is "we never go to court," the wedge dies regardless of price.**

## Gaps not closed

1. UMRA licence fee (UGX 500,000) is secondary — confirm against the Money Lenders Regulations 2018 itself.
2. Current court-fee schedule (2022 ad valorem amendment) unverified.
3. Split of the 2,132 into money lenders vs NDTMFIs vs SACCOs.
4. Latest register (figure rests on UMRA May-2023 publication).
5. **Loan default / recovery-cost data (roll rates, write-offs) — the load-bearing input on whether s.88(1) pain is real. NO VERIFIED SOURCE.**

**Bottom line:** rating holds at **3/10**. Correction: Tier 4 moves from "killed on price" to **"viable only as a low-ACV sidecar, pending proof that s.88(1) pain exists."** The price question comes **after** the pain question, not before.
