## 2. Employment (Amendment) Act 2025 — VERIFIED STATUS (update to prior docs)

- **Assented:** 29 April 2026 (DLA Piper/S&L, Afriwise, Onyango & Co., BNM).
- **Commencement:** Gazetted **5 June 2026** (ULII official publication: "Date of Commencement: 5th June, 2026"). **It IS IN FORCE as of Aug 2026.** *May-2026 law-firm alerts that said "not yet gazetted" were accurate then; that window has closed.*

**Key provisions now in force:**
- Codified **severance ~1 month per year** (new ss.86/88; "salary" base ambiguous — litigable).
- **Casual employment capped at 6 months**, with continuous-re-engagement aggregation (s.34A).
- **Probation changes** (s.66) — payment in lieu of notice extended; automatic confirmation on expiry.
- **Mandatory pre-dismissal hearing** (s.65) — 5 working days to prepare; 4 weeks' net pay penalty on non-compliance.
- **Increased unfair-dismissal compensation** (ss.76/77) — 4 → 8 weeks.
- Breach-of-duty protection, breastfeeding/childcare (s.56A), recruitment-abroad and migrant-worker Parts.
- **Penalties rose ~1,358–1,983% to UGX 10–14m/occurrence; prison 5–7 years** (BNM Advocates).

**Implication:** any Uganda build must budget for contract/probation compliance, **severance accrual** and **pre-dismissal hearings** from day one. This increases the prudent liability buffer vs the prior model.

## 3. Loaded-cost correction (2026)

**Replaced Glassdoor (n=3, rejected) with a disclosed-method survey.** MaxisHR 2026 (200+ Kampala/Entebbe employers): mid engineer UGX 3.5–6m/mo, **senior UGX 6–10m/mo**. Nucamp/Flexi corroborate (mid UGX 2.5–4m, senior local UGX 5.2–8m).

**Defensible bands:**
- Senior engineer: **UGX 6–10m/mo gross** (~UGX 8m midpoint).
- Mid engineer: **UGX 3.5–6m/mo**.
- Compliance/domain expert (ex-UMRA, ex-INGO grants/compliance): **UGX 4–7m/mo**.

**Loaded-cost multiplier ≈ 1.15–1.20x gross:** NSSF employer **10%** (employee 5%; no ceiling), Workers' Compensation premium **~0.25–0.275% of payroll** (IRA class-1, compulsory) plus admin/benefits ~3–6%. **PAYE (URA, effective 1 Jul 2026, Income Tax (Amendment) Act 2026):** 0% ≤UGX 335k/mo; 20% 335–410k; 25% 410–485k; 30% 485k–10m; **40%** >10m. NSSF is **not** a PAYE deduction. *Verify current URA bands before filing.*

**Correction note vs prior docs:** the prior "1.66:1 net wedge" and "1.11x statutory floor" remain directionally valid but the now-in-force 2025 Act and updated salary data raise the **prudent planning** multiplier toward **1.2x for provisioning and ~1.35x all-in** — consistent with the prior guidance, now with better salary anchors.

## 4. Single best vertical + biggest objection

**Best: Money lenders / UMRA Tier 4.** It uniquely combines (a) a cheap, deep, institutional-credibility pool (ex-UMRA inspectors, Tier-4 credit/MIS operators — abundant and affordable vs Big 4/professional), (b) a large base (~1,800 licensed institutions) chronically under-compliant with record-keeping, and (c) a sympathetic regulator that owns the problem. **Co-founder wise it is the most findable:** a 35–50 institutional-rolodex profile maps to veteran MFI operators and association leaders who are reachable and equity-inclined — far more than Big-4, research-ethics or judiciary staff.

**Biggest objection:** a **fragile institutional anchor.** UMRA is being rationalised/downgraded into a Ministry of Finance department, licensing discipline and enforcement are under-resourced, and a Tier 4 Amendment Bill is pending. If the regulator is neither resourced nor stable, "regulator-mandated purchase" weakens and the product becomes **voluntary opt-in**, lengthening sales cycles.
